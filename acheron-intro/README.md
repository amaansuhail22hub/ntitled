# ACHERON — "Ahead of Time" intro (4K)

Animated brand intro, 2160×3840 (9:16 UHD), 24 fps, from the locked script.
Everything is generated by code. The visuals, voiceover and sound design use no stock footage and no sample libraries.

It comes in two cuts that share every frame of the 45 s intro:
- **Site cut (48.5 s):** a 3.5 s "use headphones" card, then the full intro. For the Shopify hero and YouTube, where nobody scrolls past.
- **Reels cut (44.6 s):** built for Instagram's stay-or-scroll window. There's no card and the first 0.4 s of black is trimmed. One heartbeat is split across the ears (lub left, dub right) on frame one, with the light line flashing down the centre and a small headphones badge up top. The first words land at 0.6 s. Every voiceover line that isn't already on screen as typography gets a burned-in caption, typed on at the pace of the voice, at a height Instagram's interface doesn't cover.

**Reels deliverables:**
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_Reels_4K.mp4`: H.264, about 13 Mbps, kept in git. Upload this one to Instagram.
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_Reels_4K_HEVC.mp4`: HEVC, under 30 MB, for phones and messaging.
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_Reels_4K_master.mp4`: H.264, about 22 Mbps. Built by `./make.sh` and kept out of git (over 100 MB).

**Site deliverables** (all 2160×3840, 24 fps, BT.709, -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP):
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_4K.mp4`: H.264, about 13 Mbps (79 MB). This is the copy kept in git.
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_4K_HEVC.mp4`: HEVC, about 4.7 Mbps (28 MB). A small 4K copy for phones and messaging.
- `out/ACHERON_AheadOfTime_4K_master.mp4`: H.264, about 22 Mbps (136 MB). Upload this one to Instagram or Shopify. It's built by `./make.sh` and kept out of git because GitHub rejects files over 100 MB.

**Stems:** `out/stems/vo.wav` (voice only) and `out/stems/music_fx_no_vo.wav` (everything except the voice)

## What's in the cut (site version)

A soft low pulse plays in the left ear, then the right, in sync with the arcs, over a faint air sweep. It doubles as a real stereo check.

## Placeholders to replace before posting

## Logo

The brand files live in `assets/`:
- `acheron-logo.png`: the full horizontal lockup (mark, ACHERON, — AHEAD OF TIME —). It's split automatically by connected components into the mark, the 7 letters (for the letter-by-letter build) and the tagline row. The final lockup in scene 5/6 reproduces this file's exact layout and proportions.
- `acheron-icon.png`: the icon-only mark, taken from the brand guidelines. It's used for the dagger rising from the water and as the mark inside the lockup, because it has twice the source resolution.

Both are low-res bitmaps, so each part is upscaled through a smooth threshold (8× cubic, blur, re-threshold, area-downsample). That gives vector-sharp edges at 4K instead of blur. Swap either file and rebuild. With no files present, placeholder art is drawn.

## Placeholders to replace before posting

1. **Voice.** A TTS placeholder: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), a blend of two low male voices (`am_michael` 70%, `am_onyx` 30%). There's no pitch shifting, saturation or added noise; all three made it sound robotic. The chain is just warm EQ, treble rolled off above about 4 kHz (−7 dB shelf plus an 8 kHz low-pass), gentle compression and a dark cathedral tail. "Acheron" is forced to /ˈækəɹɑːn/ ("A-kuh-ron"). For the human read, record each line as its own take, save as `build/vo_human/00.wav` … `20.wav` (line order = `timeline.VO`), and run `./make.sh --human`. Each take is trimmed and placed on its cue automatically.
2. **Drop date.** "NEW YEAR'S." is the date stand-in. Change it in `timeline.py` (`INVITE`) and the VO line 20.

## Rebuild

```bash
pip install numpy scipy opencv-python-headless pillow soundfile kokoro-onnx
# TTS model (only needed for the placeholder voice), ~350 MB:
mkdir -p build/models && cd build/models
curl -LO https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/download/model-files-v1.0/kokoro-v1.0.onnx
curl -LO https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/download/model-files-v1.0/voices-v1.0.bin
cd ../.. && ./make.sh
```

`python3 render.py --scale 0.5 --stills 1.9,14.1,31.8,41.0` dumps quick preview frames to `build/stills/` (file time, card included).
A full 4K render takes about 8 minutes on 4 cores, plus about 15 minutes for the encodes.
